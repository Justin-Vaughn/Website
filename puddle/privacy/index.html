Puddle Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

The short version: Puddle has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and never sells your data. Your location is used to get your forecast, kept only as a general area, and never linked to you or your device.

What Puddle sends to our server, and why

  • Your approximate location, to get your forecast. Your exact position never leaves your phone, and we only store it as a general area of about 10 km.
  • Which weather data the app asked for, so we can count how the service is used.
  • A random security key that helps confirm requests come from the Puddle app. It isn't your device's advertising identifier, and it isn't connected to your name, Apple Account or location.
  • Your Puddle Plus status. If you subscribe, the app sends Apple's record of your subscription so we can confirm it. We check it and don't keep it. We never see your payment details; Apple handles all payments.

What we keep, and for how long

WhatWhyHow long
A record of each request: the time, the general area and the data asked forTo run and improve the service90 days
Forecasts for each areaTo provide the serviceNot linked to anyone
The security keyTo keep the service secureUntil it's gone unused for 90 days

We don't keep anything that links a location to a device or a person for more than about an hour, and we don't store IP addresses.

What stays on your iPhone

Your saved places, settings, card layout, and game streaks and badges are stored only on your iPhone. We never receive them.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-up, names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no sharing with data brokers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting code from other companies in the app.
  • We never sell your data.

Services Puddle uses

  • Apple, for place search, the forecast when our server can't be reached, App Store purchases and app security, under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • The US National Weather Service, which the app asks directly for official watches and warnings where you are, sending your location.
  • Weather data providers, which our server gets forecast data from. They never receive anything from your phone.
  • Cloudflare, which carries traffic to our server and sees your IP address in order to deliver it, under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Puddle asks for your location so it can show your local weather. You can turn location access off at any time in the Settings app; you can still search for places. Because we don't keep anything that identifies you, we can't look up or delete data about a particular person, but request records are deleted automatically after 90 days. If you have a question about your data, email us.

Children

Puddle isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
