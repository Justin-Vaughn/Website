Puddle Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

The short version: Puddle has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and never sells your data. Your location is used to get your forecast and check for warnings, kept only as a general area or not at all, and never linked to you or your device. The one exception is notifications: if you turn them on, our server keeps the places you want them for, linked to a random ID for your device, until you turn them off.

What Puddle sends to our server, and why

  • Your approximate location, to get your forecast. For forecasts, your exact position never leaves your phone, and we only store it as a general area of about 10 km.
  • Your location, to check for official warnings. This one is exact, because a warning's boundary can fall between you and the middle of your area. Our server passes it to the National Weather Service and doesn't store it: the answer for that spot is held in the server's memory for about a minute, so checking again doesn't ask twice, and it's never written to our database or linked to your device.
  • Which weather data the app asked for, so we can count how the service is used.
  • A random security key that helps confirm requests come from the Puddle app. It isn't your device's advertising identifier, and it isn't connected to your name, Apple Account or location.
  • Your Puddle Plus status. If you subscribe, the app sends Apple's record of your subscription so we can confirm it. We check it and don't keep it. We never see your payment details; Apple handles all payments.

If you turn on notifications

Notifications are off until you turn them on in Puddle's settings. To send them, our server needs to know where to look and how to reach your iPhone, so while they're on it keeps:

  • A push token, the address Apple gives your iPhone for Puddle's notifications, and a random ID the app makes for your install. Neither is your name, Apple Account or advertising identifier.
  • The places you want notifications for: where you are, if you turn that on, and any saved places you choose, with their names. These are exact locations, so we can check the weather there. If you let Puddle use your location in the background, where you are is updated as you move.
  • Your notification settings: which kinds you want, the notifications you build, quiet hours, your units and clock (so notifications read the way the app does), the app version and whether you have Puddle Plus.
  • Which warnings we've already sent you, so you don't get the same one twice, and when other notifications last went out at each place, so you don't get repeats or too many in a day.
  • A copy of each notification we send you: its title, its text and when it was sent, so you can look back at it in Puddle's Notification Center.

Turning notifications off in Puddle deletes all of this from our server. We use it only to send your notifications, and never to track you or build a profile.

What stays on your iPhone

Your saved places, settings, card layout, and game streaks and badges are stored only on your iPhone. We never receive them, except the places and settings you choose for notifications, as described above.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-up, names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no sharing with data brokers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting code from other companies in the app.
  • We never sell your data.

Services Puddle uses

  • Apple, for place search, the forecast when our server can't be reached, App Store purchases and app security, under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Push Notification service, which delivers notifications to your iPhone. Apple receives your push token and each notification's text in order to deliver it.
  • The US National Weather Service, which our server asks for official watches and warnings at the location the app sends, without anything about you or your device. If our server can't be reached, the app asks the National Weather Service directly, sending your location. For notifications, our server also asks it about the general areas being watched.
  • Weather data providers, which our server gets forecast data from. They never receive anything from your phone.
  • Cloudflare, which carries traffic to our server and sees your IP address in order to deliver it, under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Puddle asks for your location so it can show your local weather. You can turn location access off at any time in the Settings app; you can still search for places. You can turn notifications off in Puddle's settings at any time, which deletes your notification data from our server. If you turn them off in the Settings app instead, Puddle deletes it the next time it opens. Because we don't keep anything that identifies you, we can't look up or delete data about a particular person, but request records are deleted automatically after 90 days. If you have a question about your data, email us.

Children

Puddle isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
